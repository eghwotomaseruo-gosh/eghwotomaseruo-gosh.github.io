I'm a full-stack developer building Lingua Naija — a gamified app that teaches Nigeria's three major languages: Igbo, Yoruba, and Hausa. It works the way Duolingo does: XP, streaks, hearts, leaderboards, and badges, with real user accounts and progress that's actually saved.

My background is in electrical and electronics engineering — circuits, systems, and telecommunications gave me the technical foundation that eventually pulled me toward software development.

I'm passionate about using technology to preserve and promote African languages and culture, and I'm always glad to connect with other developers, educators, and anyone working on African language tech.
